# v0.4.2: Needs You lists only turns waiting on you

Source-only release; no prebuilt app is attached. Install or update with
Homebrew once the tap moves to 0.4.2 (`brew install silex-ai-lab/tap/vibra`, or
`brew upgrade silex-ai-lab/tap/vibra`), or build from source with the
[installation instructions](https://github.com/silex-ai-lab/silex_vibra/tree/v0.4.2#install).

## Fixed

- **A finished job no longer asks for you.** A Codex app scheduled task's run,
  and a Codex or Claude Code agent-fleet seat that finished a `Dispatch <id>:`
  prompt, showed under **Needs You** as "your turn", though no one was expected
  to reply. Vibra now reads who started the turn. Codex records it in
  `task_started.turn_attribution.turn_trigger`. A fleet planner's prompt opens
  with `Dispatch <id>:`, the convention the orca and herdr fleet skills use.
  These turns now end idle. A turn you started still shows as your turn.

## Changed

- **A "your turn" you have dealt with clears.** Click a Needs You row or its
  notification and land in the session, and that turn is marked handled. It
  drops to idle at once, stays idle across a relaunch, and comes back only
  when the session has new activity: you replied, or the agent wrote again. An
  approval prompt is never cleared this way. It stays until it is answered.
- **An unanswered "your turn" fades after 2 h, not 8 h.** The Settings window
  keeps the 1 h to 48 h range. A value you set before is kept.

## What Vibra now stores

The handled marks are stored in the existing preferences domain under the key
`handledTurns`: one session id and one timestamp per mark. Marks are dropped
once their session has moved on or is gone. `defaults delete ai.silexlab.vibra`
removes them with the other preferences. Vibra still makes no network request.

## Verification

- CI builds the app, runs all 513 tests and launches the app on the macOS 15
  runners with Swift 6.1.2, on Apple Silicon and Intel.
- `make test SWIFT_BUILD_SYSTEM=native`: 513 tests in 61 suites passed on
  macOS 27 with Swift 6.4, including the four privacy canaries.
- The turn-owner tests were written first. Before the fix, the three cases
  that should end idle failed with `.turnComplete`. With the handled rule
  broken on purpose, two of its tests fail.
- On the development Mac's live sessions, `--dump-menu` moved an automation
  run and two fleet seats from Needs You to Recent. A turn typed by a human
  stayed. A handled mark written under a throwaway preferences domain moved
  that turn to Recent too.

## Limits still open

- Going back to a session outside Vibra (switching to its terminal yourself)
  is not seen. That turn clears when you reply or when it fades.
- A fleet seat is recognized only by the `Dispatch <id>:` prompt convention. A
  planner that phrases its prompts differently still shows its seats as your
  turn.
- The app is ad-hoc signed, not Developer ID signed or notarized, so
  notifications need one manual step in System Settings.
